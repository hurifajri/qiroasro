# The Noble Quran

The Noble Quran (Al-Quran Al-Karim) is the holy book of Islam, revealed by Allah to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ over a period of 23 years through the Angel Jibreel (Gabriel). It is the primary source of Islamic law, guidance, and spirituality.

## Revelation

The Quran was revealed in **Arabic** — the language of the Arab people at the time — beginning with the first verses of Surah Al-'Alaq in 610 CE, and concluding near the end of the Prophet's life ﷺ.

> "Indeed, it is We who sent down the Quran and indeed, We will be its guardian."
> — Surah Al-Hijr [15:9]

## Structure

The Quran is divided into:

- **114 Surahs** (chapters), ranging from 3 to 286 verses
- **6,236 Ayat** (verses)
- **30 Juz'** (parts) — used for dividing the Quran for daily recitation
- **60 Hizb** (portions) — further subdivisions of each Juz'

## Preservation

Unlike earlier scriptures, the Quran has been miraculously preserved in its original form since revelation. Millions of Muslims have memorized the entire Quran by heart — these individuals are called **Huffaz** (plural of Hafiz).

The first official written compilation was ordered by Abu Bakr As-Siddiq (ra), and the standardized written copies (Masahif) were distributed during the caliphate of Uthman ibn Affan (ra).

## Recitation

Reciting the Quran with proper pronunciation and rules (Tajweed) is considered an act of worship. The Prophet ﷺ said:

> "The one who is proficient in the recitation of the Quran will be with the honorable and obedient scribes (angels), and the one who recites the Quran and finds it difficult, will have a double reward."
> — Sahih Al-Bukhari & Muslim

## The Seven Ahruf

The Quran was revealed in **seven ahruf** (modes/dialects) to facilitate recitation by different Arab tribes. Today, the **ten canonical qira'at** (recitation modes) are accepted, all tracing back to the original revelation.

## Learning the Quran

To properly recite the Quran, one must study:

1. **Makhraj** — Points of articulation for each Arabic letter
2. **Sifat** — Characteristics of each letter
3. **Tajweed rules** — Rules for proper recitation
4. **Waqf & Ibtida'** — Rules for stopping and starting

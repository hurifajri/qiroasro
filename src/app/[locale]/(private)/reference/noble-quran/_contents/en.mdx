# Definition of the Quran

## Table of Contents

The Quran is the word (kalam) of Allah <Arabic>سُبْحَانَهُ وَ تَعَالَى</Arabic> revealed directly into the heart of Prophet Muhammad <Arabic>صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ</Arabic>. Reciting it is considered an act of worship, its text is compiled between the two covers of the mushaf, and it has reached us through a *mutawatir* chain of transmission. Furthermore, the Quran is a miracle that challenges everyone (both humans and jinn) to produce even a single shortest surah like it.

## The Prophet's Method of Transmitting the Quran to His Ummah

The Messenger of Allah <Arabic>صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ</Arabic> left and transmitted the Quran to the Muslim ummah through two main methods:
1. **In written form**, through recording the text of the revelation into writing.
2. **In oral form**, through vocal transmission or memorization.

## Meaning of Mutawatir

*Mutawatir* is the process of transmitting information by a massive number of narrators who are widely dispersed in every generation, from the beginning of the chain of transmission (sanad) to its end. Logically, it is impossible for such a massive number of narrators to conspire to fabricate a narration. It is through this *mutawatir* route that the Quran—both in written and oral form—has reached us perfectly preserved.

## The Process of Oral Transmission of the Quran

The process of transmitting the Quran vocally went through strict stages:
1. The Angel Jibril brought down the revelation of the Quran into the heart of Prophet Muhammad <Arabic>صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ</Arabic>, complete with its wording, meaning, and all rules of its recitation.
2. The noble companions learned the recitation of the Quran directly from the lips of the Messenger of Allah <Arabic>صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ</Arabic>. They then recited it back from memory in front of him to receive correction and validation of their recitation.
3. The companions then taught and transmitted the recitation to the next generation using the exact same method. This chain of teaching has continued uninterrupted until the Quran reached us today.

## Phases of the Codification of the Quran

The history of writing and compiling the Quran went through five important phases:
1. **Recording every segment immediately in the presence of the Messenger of Allah <Arabic>صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ</Arabic> when the revelation occurred.** Zaid bin Thabit <Arabic>رَضِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ عَنْهُ</Arabic> said:
<ArabicBlock>كُنتُ أكتب الوحي عِندَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ وهو يُملي علي فإذا فرغتُ قال : اقرأ ، فأَقرَؤُه، فإن كان فيه سقط أقامه ، ثم أخرُجُ بِهِ إِلَى النَّاسِ</ArabicBlock>
> I used to write down the revelation in the presence of the Messenger of Allah <Arabic>صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ</Arabic> while he dictated it to me. When I finished, he would say: 'Read it.' So I would read it. If there was any omission in it, he would correct it, then I would bring it out to the people.

(Narrated by At-Tabarani with a chain of reliable narrators)

2. **Compilation during the time of Abu Bakr as-Siddiq <Arabic>رَضِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ عَنْهُ</Arabic>.** The scattered records of revelation were collected and copied into unified sheets (*suhuf*).
3. **Writing of the Mushaf during the time of Uthman bin Affan <Arabic>رَضِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ عَنْهُ</Arabic>.** The sheets from Abu Bakr's <Arabic>رَضِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ عَنْهُ</Arabic> time were recopied into several standard mushafs. Caliph Uthman <Arabic>رَضِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ عَنْهُ</Arabic> then sent copies of these mushafs to various Islamic regions, accompanied by an expert reciter (*qari*) to teach the Muslims how to recite it.
4. **Mass copying by the Muslims.** Muslims across the regions began copying these standard mushafs in countless numbers.
5. **The emergence of the science of mushaf orthography.** Over time, various literature and scientific rules emerged specifically regulating the writing of the Quran (Rasmul Quran).
